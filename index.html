SINCE 1997 • HERITAGE IN DIGITAL

A Journey Through Sound & Silence

Ribkha's narrative is one of discipline and melodic exploration. Her foundation was laid in the quiet halls of SD Joannes Bosco, where her voice first found its resonance.

This path continued through SMP Budi Utama, honing the technical precision required for international stages, and ultimately culminating in the artistic maturity found at SMA Stella Duce 1.

Today, she stands at the intersection of heritage and innovation—a singer whose reach is global, but whose heart remains rooted in the artisanal roots of her education.
